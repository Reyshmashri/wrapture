## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the convergence of high-end industrial craftsmanship and computational intelligence. It repudiates sterile SaaS conventions and cliché ecological aesthetics—dispensing with neon greens, simplistic leaf motifs, and mass-market minimalism. Instead, it projects the quiet authority of an architectural atelier or bespoke paper mill.

The target audience encompasses luxury brand directors, industrial packaging engineers, and high-growth circular economy founders who value material tactile precision as much as structural telemetry. The interface evokes unboxing museum-grade artifacts: measured, tactile, composed, and technologically rigorous.

### Visual Aesthetic
- **Editorial Organicism**: High-contrast, literary display typography set against asymmetric, mathematically paced grids.
- **Physical Materiality**: Tonal layering evocative of unbleached cardstock, pressed linen fibers, and raw terracotta pigment.
- **Atmospheric Quietude**: Ample, deliberate negative space punctuated by sharp hairline separators and restrained contextual metrics.

## Colors

The palette establishes an earthy, bespoke atmosphere dominated by warm cellulose and vegetal pigments, anchored by deep forest undertones.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Canvas (`#FAF7F1` - Warm Ivory)**: Forms the primary plane. Provides an organic, natural paper feel with zero sterile blue undertones.
- **Surfaces & Receptacles (`#ECE2D4` - Sand & `#D7CAA6` - Clay Beige)**: Applied to secondary planes, structural shelves, card panels, and modal containers.
- **Primary Accent (`#2E4A3A` - Olive Forest)**: The primary structural ink. Used for authoritative typography, critical CTAs, active toggles, and solid brand markers.
- **Secondary Accent (`#8CAA88` - Sage Green)**: Conveys computational telemetry, material lifecycle confirmation, and soft state indicators.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#B67B58` - Terracotta)**: Used sparingly as an artisan mark—indicating urgent alerts, structural stress points in CAD simulations, or bespoke interactive callouts.
- **Text & Contrast (`#2B2B28` - Charcoal & `#7A7568` - Muted Taupe)**: Text is grounded in deep graphite rather than pure black, softening contrast while maintaining WCAG AAA legibility. Secondary labels and specs leverage muted taupe to create editorial rhythm.

## Typography

The typographical pairing sets up a deliberate dialogue between classical artisanal publishing and technical precision.

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Expressive, high-contrast serif with sculpted terminals. Used for editorial lead-ins, brand headers, and prominent narrative moments. It gives voice to the sensory, structural quality of packaging.
- **Body & Metrics (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Clean, geometric sans-serif tuned for legibility at small sizes. Used across packaging specifications, algorithmic outputs, and structural dimensions.
- **Label Treatment**: Category markers, sustainability ratings, and engineering parameters are consistently rendered in `label-caps` (all-caps, tracking widened to `0.08em`, semi-bold) to evoke archival cataloging and technical blueprint documentation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy uses a disciplined 12-column architectural grid on desktop, scaling to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

### Rhythm & Proportion
- **Asymmetric Composition**: Large display imagery or interactive 3D package models occupy wider asymmetrical spans (e.g., 7 columns against 5 columns of engineering data), preventing a cookie-cutter dashboard look.
- **Whitespace Rules**: Outer canvas margins remain generous (`margin` = `3rem`) to frame the application like a monograph. Dense technical dashboards scale margin down gracefully without collapsing padding between functional modules.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - `Desktop`: 1280px and above. Full 12-column presentation with deliberate asymmetric whitespace.
  - `Tablet`: 768px – 1279px. 6-column grid; peripheral sidebars collapse into drawer sheets.
  - `Mobile`: Below 768px. Single-column stacked hierarchy; hero editorial titles scale down dynamically to preserve proportion.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than relying on generic drop shadows, depth is achieved through structural materiality, subtle surface shifts, and hairline fiber boundaries.

### Tactile Layering System
1. **Base Sheet (`Level 0`)**: `#FAF7F1` (Warm Ivory). The foundation of all views.
2. **Cut Panels (`Level 1`)**: `#ECE2D4` (Sand) bounded by a hairline border: `1px solid rgba(215, 202, 166, 0.45)`. Zero shadow; separation is achieved strictly by value contrast and precision line work.
3. **Elevated Trays (`Level 2`)**: Used for modal windows, parameter adjusters, and floating material swatches. Employs an ambient shadow tinted with warm umber: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(43, 43, 40, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(43, 43, 40, 0.04)`.
4. **Structural Die-Cut Insets (`Recessed`)**: Recessed metric fields and input wells utilize a micro inner shadow: `inset 0 1px 2px rgba(43, 43, 40, 0.05)` layered on top of `#D7CAA6` at 20% opacity.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a soft, architectural restraint (`Level 1`). 

- Standard inputs, buttons, and sub-panels feature a `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius, recalling the clean, stamped edges of heavy unbent kraft cardstock.
- Floating sheets and primary structural cards feature `0.5rem` (8px) corners.
- Circular or pill geometries are reserved exclusively for contextual status chips and tactile toggle pins.
- Avoid large, bulbous border radii that compromise the precision-engineered feel.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#2E4A3A` (Olive Forest) with text in `#FAF7F1`. Corners `0.25rem`. Padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Subtle hover transitions shift the surface to `#3B5E4A` with zero scale pop.
- **Secondary (Crafted)**: Outline button styled with `1px solid rgba(46, 74, 58, 0.35)`, surface `#FAF7F1`, text `#2E4A3A`. Hover transitions to background `#ECE2D4`.
- **Tertiary Accent**: Terracotta `#B67B58` background reserved for irrevocable actions or definitive simulation triggers.

### Chips & Badges
- Low profile, rendered in `label-caps`. 
- Certified Bio-based chips use a background of `rgba(140, 170, 136, 0.2)` with text `#2E4A3A` and a hairline border of `rgba(140, 170, 136, 0.4)`.

### Cards & Packaging Panels
- Built on `#ECE2D4` (Sand) backgrounds with hairline borders (`rgba(215, 202, 166, 0.5)`).
- Asymmetrical layout structure: visual renders or die-line previews sit in the top/left compartment, while technical specs (tensile strength, biodegradation rate) align via a tabular baseline grid.

### Form Inputs
- Background `#FAF7F1`, border `1px solid #D7CAA6`, corners `0.25rem`. 
- Active focus state: border shifts to `#2E4A3A` with an ambient ring: `0 0 0 1px #2E4A3A`.
- Placeholder text styled in `#7A7568` with standard tracking.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square with `2px` micro-radius for checkboxes; crisp circles for radios.
- Inactive: Border `1.5px solid #7A7568` on warm canvas.
- Active: Fill `#2E4A3A` with sharp ivory glyph check mark.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Material Lifecycle Gauge**: A continuous, segmented line meter illustrating fiber composition (Virgin Wood Pulp, Agricultural Waste, Post-Consumer Recycled). Uses `#2E4A3A`, `#8CAA88`, and `#D7CAA6` segments with tiny hairline dividers.
- **Die-Cut Structural Inspector**: A blueprint inspection viewport with subtle graph rules (`rgba(104, 79, 58, 0.08)`) supporting CAD and folding simulation outputs.
